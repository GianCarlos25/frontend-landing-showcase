Our Story

Born from a Search
for Authentic Rest

Aura was founded in 2018 by Mia Chen and Rajan Patel, after a year traveling through India and Bali, studying under masters of various lineages. They returned to New York with one conviction: genuine wellness shouldn't feel clinical. It should feel like coming home.

Today, our studio on the Upper West Side hosts over 80 classes each week, taught by instructors who have spent years—not months—deepening their practice. We are a place of rigor and softness. Of discipline and grace.
